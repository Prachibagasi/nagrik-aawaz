About Us

Department of School Education, Uttarakhand

The state of Uttarakhand was carved out of Uttar Pradesh on November 9, 2000. Uttarakhand was a centre of education from ancient times — it was believed that the Kauravas and Pandavas were trained by Guru Dronacharya in the foothills of the Himalayas, which is why Dehradun is also known as Drona Nagari.

India's best schools and training institutes are located in Uttarakhand. The Department of Education has a unified structure covering basic education, secondary education, SCERT, Uttarakhand Board of School Education — all working under the Directorate of School Education.
