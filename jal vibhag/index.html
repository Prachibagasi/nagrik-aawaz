              About Department

              "Uttarakhand Jal Sansthan" constituted under Section 18 of the Principal Act having jurisdiction throughout the state of Uttarakhand by amalgamation of "Garhwal Jal Sansthan" and "Kumaun Jal Sansthan" on 26th August 2002. It extends to the whole of Uttarakhand excluding cantonment areas. The secretary of Pey Jal Vibhag is the chairman of Uttarakhand Jal Sansthan.
